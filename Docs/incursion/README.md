# FSHING incursion submission pack

This folder contains the source evidence used to prepare the Year 10 Computing Game Design Incursion materials.

## Final deliverables

- `output/pdf/FSHING-Incursion-GDD.docx` - editable, print-ready game design document
- `output/pdf/FSHING-Incursion-GDD.pdf` - 16-page print copy
- `output/presentation/FSHING-Elevator-Pitch.pptx` - editable six-slide pitch with speaker notes
- `output/presentation/FSHING-Elevator-Pitch.pdf` - portable presentation copy

## Evidence and asset policy

- `screenshots/` contains fresh captures from the local `v0.9.0 (PR #114)` build at 1600 x 900.
- `assets/working-boat-hero.png` is a supporting portfolio illustration generated with OpenAI's built-in image-generation tool. It is based on `src/assets/player-boat.png` and `src/assets/lake-chart.png`. It is not presented as gameplay and is not imported by the game.
- The production FSHING wordmark, logo, and icon atlas are reused from `src/assets/`.
- The GDD's peer-feedback and feedback-to-change pages are intentionally blank. They are designed to record evidence during and after the incursion rather than inventing feedback before it happens.

## Image-generation prompt

Use case: `stylized-concept`. Supporting main-character hero illustration for the FSHING game design document and classroom elevator-pitch deck. Preserve the production boat's dark teal hull, cream wheelhouse, square blue window, round porthole, stern cargo crate, working hook crane, roof lantern, weathering, proportions, and right-facing side profile. Match the project's restrained editorial gouache and screen-print palette on a warm cream paper field. Include one complete boat only. No text, labels, logos, people, fish, extra boats, UI, waterline, scenery, or watermark.

## Checklist coverage

The final GDD includes the elevator pitch, main-character description, game concept and learning purpose, target-audience justification, gameplay mechanics and rules, storyboard, screen flow and wireframe decisions, visual/audio design, asset planning, planning/design/reflection sections, testing and fixing, existing feedback, and blank incursion feedback records.
