# FSHING

FSHING is a fishing-market game about choosing a catch from the Fish market, landing it at a fishing ground, and selling it at the stronger harbor.

## Language

**Fishing session**:
The period from dropping the line at a fishing ground until the player returns to cruising, including the dive, hook steering, line fight, landing or line break, and return to the surface.
_Avoid_: Fishing mode, fishing sequence
